Full description:

Audio Desk brings audio industry headlines and production resources into a
compact, retro-styled reader.

- Browse news about plugins, recording tools, sound design and post-production.
- Discover REAPER scripts and repositories.
- Filter headlines by topic and search news sources.
- Save article summaries on your device and return to the publisher for the full
  story. Previously cached headlines remain available when refresh requests fail.
- Explore a directory of sample libraries and sound resources, including free
  and paid sources. Each publisher's licensing terms apply.
- Add your preferred RSS feeds and remove sources you do not want to follow.

An internet connection is needed to fetch new headlines and open publisher
websites. Coverage depends on the availability of external publishers, RSS2JSON,
Google News, Bing News and GitHub. Audio Desk is an independent discovery tool;
it is not affiliated with those services or the manufacturers mentioned in feeds.
