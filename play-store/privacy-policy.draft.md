## Information stored on your device

Audio Desk stores cached headline summaries, saved article details, selected RSS
sources and removed-source settings in the app's local WebView storage. Clearing
app storage or uninstalling the app removes this local information. The current
Android manifest disables Android app-data backup.

## Network requests and third-party services

To load feeds, the app sends RSS feed URLs to RSS2JSON. News search terms are
embedded in Google News or Bing News feed URLs sent through that service.
REAPER repository discovery sends search requests to GitHub. Fonts, article
images and publisher pages are requested from external providers. These network
requests disclose ordinary connection information, such as the IP address and
HTTP request metadata, to the services receiving them.

Opening a publisher page loads that website in an in-app browser. Its scripts,
cookies, storage and information practices are controlled by that publisher.
Review the policies of the services and websites you use. Do not place private
credentials or confidential information in custom feed URLs or search fields.

The current app source contains no developer-operated account service, advertising
SDK or analytics SDK. This does not mean that external websites and services
collect no information. The owner must review those services' current practices
before completing Google's Data safety declaration.

## Your choices

You can remove feeds, unsave articles and clear the app's local storage in Android
settings. Loading new headlines and external pages requires network access.
Requests already received by third parties are subject to their policies and
cannot be erased by clearing the app's local storage.
